\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Primitive collider generation should be closed by physics-engine diagnostics before packages are trusted in Newton workflows.
Our current evidence demonstrates that simulation-checked acceptance can expose failures invisible to geometry-only fitting, provides a guard prototype, and records a scoped performance hook for accepted primitives.
Completing the Phase~0 benchmark and robot articulation gates is required before broader claims or venue transfer versions substitute for the ACCV primary draft.
