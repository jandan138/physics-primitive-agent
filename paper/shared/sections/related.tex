\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Primitive and convex collision proxies.}
Manual primitives, single convex hulls, and decomposition methods such as CoACD, V-HACD, and CPD-style primitive fitting provide candidate collision geometry with different fidelity--complexity trade-offs.
We treat them as generators or baselines, not as the novelty claim of this work.

\paragraph{Physics-engine validation.}
Simulators expose contact, penetration, and body-state behavior that geometric fitting cannot see.
Our framing is a diagnostic checker under named Newton tasks and settings, not formal verification or safety certification.

\paragraph{Robot collision authoring.}
Articulated assets require link-aware packages: primitives must not merge across joints, and articulation smoke checks are prerequisites for any whole-robot claim.
Current Franka evidence in this repository is capped package and task-smoke evidence only.
