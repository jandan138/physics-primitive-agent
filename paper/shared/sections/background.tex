\section{Background}
\label{sec:background}

\paragraph{Primitive packages as compound bodies.}
When multiple primitives attach to one rigid body, simulators aggregate mass properties for the compound.
A single large flat primitive can shift center of mass and inertia enough to change drop/settle behavior even if the primitive geometry alone is admissible.

\paragraph{Newton diagnostics.}
We use named probes---drop/settle, contact stress, collision-only throughput, and robot articulation smokes---as acceptance signals.
Each probe records Newton version, environment, seeds, and pass/fail labels; stronger validation wording requires a documented verification standard and broader coverage than the current Phase~0 subset.

\paragraph{Claim scope.}
We prefer ``\checker{}'' over formal-verification language and ``\simchecked{}'' only when a dated record links a package to a named task, asset, settings, and report.
