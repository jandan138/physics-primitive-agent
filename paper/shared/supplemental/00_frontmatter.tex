\paragraph{Scope.}
The main paper is self-contained. This supplementary material is a reviewer-facing explanatory
companion for the scoped diagnostic checker. The supplement uses figures that are
not copies of main-paper figures and tables that are not copies of main-paper tables. Its examples
are explanatory views of simulation-checked artifacts rather than new benchmark claims. The figures
show predicate anatomy, scene context, package accounting, link ownership, and provenance
flow; the labels, metrics, and claim boundaries remain tied to configs, records, and manifests.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
This supplement provides additional definitions, derivations, and visual explanations. It is
not new experimental evidence, not whole-robot collision quality evidence, not manipulation evidence,
not deployment readiness evidence, and not safety certification evidence.
