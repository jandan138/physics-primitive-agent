\paragraph{Scope.}
The main paper is self-contained. This supplementary material is a reviewer-facing tutorial and
provenance companion for the scoped diagnostic checker. The supplement uses figures that are
not copies of main-paper figures and tables that are not copies of main-paper tables. Its examples
are explanatory views of simulation-checked artifacts rather than new benchmark claims.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
This supplement provides additional definitions, derivations, and visual explanations. It is
not whole-robot collision quality evidence, not manipulation evidence, not deployment readiness
evidence, and not safety certification evidence.
