\section{Diagnostic Predicate Derivations}

The checker converts a simulated probe trajectory into named predicate terms. For a drop/settle
probe with final speed $v_T$, final height $z_T$, and floor height $z_f$, the schematic acceptance
form is
\[
  A_{\mathrm{drop}} =
  [\|v_T\| \leq \epsilon_v] \land [z_T \geq z_f-\epsilon_z] \land
  [\Delta z_{\mathrm{descent}} > 0].
\]
This predicate is diagnostic: it catches obvious body-state and contact failures under the recorded
scene settings, but it does not certify general physical behavior.

For stack-or-slide probes, the checker compares terminal contact, horizontal drift, and residual
speed:
\[
  A_{\mathrm{stack}} =
  [c_T = 1] \land [\|\pi_{xy}(x_T-x_0)\| \leq \epsilon_{xy}]
  \land [\|v_T\| \leq \epsilon_v].
\]
The exact thresholds live in configuration and records rather than being hardcoded into prose.
