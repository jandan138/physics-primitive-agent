\section{Supplementary Reviewer Guide}

This supplement is organized as a technical atlas. Sections~2--5 teach the notation, diagnostic
predicates, compound body-state mechanism, and link-aware robot package semantics used by the main
paper. Sections~6--8 collect additional visual explanations, implementation notes, and limitation
checks.

\paragraph{How to read the material.}
The equations are intended to make the diagnostic acceptance criteria auditable. The visual plates
are explanatory compositions; they are designed to show what the checker measures and where the
claim boundary stops.
