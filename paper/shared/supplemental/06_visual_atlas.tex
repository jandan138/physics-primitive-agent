\section{Visual Evidence Atlas}

The atlas pages will use supplement-only figures with file names under
\texttt{figures/generated/supplement/}. Each figure will show a new view, a new annotation scheme,
or a new tutorial decomposition of an existing diagnostic concept.

\paragraph{What this shows.}
The intended role of each plate is to make recorded checker behavior easier to inspect.

\paragraph{What this does not show.}
The plates do not introduce benchmark superiority, deployment claims, or unrecorded physical
coverage.
