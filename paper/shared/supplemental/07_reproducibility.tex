\section{Implementation and Reproducibility Notes}

Small paper artifacts are committed when they are needed for deterministic builds. Raw assets,
large render directories, videos, and logs remain outside the repository; the paper records them by
anonymous artifact type, compact manifest, and dated record where appropriate.

\paragraph{Build commands.}
Use the ACCV supplement target from the paper directory for the supplementary PDF. The companion
ACCV target builds the main paper and the supplementary material together.
